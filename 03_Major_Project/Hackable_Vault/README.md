# Hackable Combination Vault

## Overview

The Hackable Combination Vault was the major personal project developed during my mechatronics internship at Tobotics Pvt. Ltd.

The project combines embedded programming, electronic interfacing and mechanical CAD design to create an interactive vault concept. The intended interaction is based on entering numerical guesses and receiving feedback before a successful guess triggers a physical unlocking action.

## Project Evolution

The project evolved through several stages:

**Art Thief Vault → Syblin Concept → Mechanical Studies → Hackable Vault**

The initial Art Thief Vault explored password-based interaction, keypad input, display feedback and servo-controlled movement.

The project was then explored through the Syblin concept, which introduced a more complex mechanical character with moving teeth. After evaluating the mechanical complexity and space requirements, the design direction was changed to the Hackable Vault.

The Hackable Vault retained the core idea of password-driven physical interaction while using a more practical mechanical concept.

## Main Areas of Development

### Electronics and Embedded Systems

- Arduino-based control
- Keypad input
- Display feedback
- LEDs and buzzer
- Servo actuation
- Wokwi simulation

### Mechanical Design

- Vault enclosure
- Four-knob front-panel mechanism
- Cam-ring and follower concept
- Hinged front panel
- Door movement and locking studies
- Servo integration

### CAD and Prototyping

Fusion 360 was used to develop and study the mechanical components and assemblies.

## Current Status

The Hackable Vault remains an **in-progress prototype**.

The major electronic architecture and simulation work were developed, while the main front-panel mechanism and hinged enclosure were substantially explored in CAD.

Complete final software and full mechanical integration were not finished during the internship.
